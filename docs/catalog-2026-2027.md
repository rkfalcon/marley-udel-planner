## Changes

The SLP concentration now has eight requirements (24–25 credits). CGSC 350 replaces the former CGSC 375/376 requirement pair. Those older courses still exist in the course catalog and historical records remain intact.

Core psychology accepts PSYC 100 or NSCI 100. Statistics accepts PSYC 209 or STAT 200; MATH 202, MATH 205, and SOCI 301 no longer count automatically or through stale saved assignments. Advanced cognition adds CGSC 402 and NSCI 320 and removes CGSC 410 and PSYC 350. Verified cross-listed equivalents are included.

Biology requires BISC 104 or a complete lecture/lab combination: 207+217, 103+113, or 208+218. BISC 207 now defaults to three credits; lab courses default to one. Existing enrolled credits are not rewritten.

All 48 current CGSC entries are available in course selection, including variable-credit defaults and current titles. Newly listed Second Writing options are added to the curated course selection. The full eligibility code list includes courses rendered as plain text on the catalog page, not just hyperlinks. Section designation, prior earned credits, and grades still need confirmation. The picker is a curated planning catalog, not an import of every university course.

The total degree minimum remains 124 credits. The prior arbitrary 30-credit free-elective requirement is displayed as the degree credit minimum. Requirement counts change from 32 to 31 because the SLP requirement pair is now one requirement.

## Record and plan behavior

No production academic records or saved plan documents are rewritten by this release. Shared requirement evaluation applies the new rules to the dashboard, requirements, existing plans, new plans, graduation tracking, and print views. Canonical academic record reconciliation remains in place. Legacy requirement IDs remain valid for saving historical data; an existing CGSC 350 assigned to the old CGSC 375 slot is recognized under the new requirement and normalized when edited.

The site identifies this as its planning catalog. It does not change Marley's official catalog assignment at UDel. Grade minima, approved substitutions, and designated Second Writing sections must be checked against the official audit with her advisor. Grades remain informational; recorded completion status controls earned credit.

## Verification

Regression tests cover the new concentration, paired biology labs, changed statistics and advanced options, cross-listed courses, variable-credit minima, legacy assignments, and unlinked Second Writing entries. Existing tests cover admin persistence, completion, shared state, and saved plan reconciliation.

Browser verification used an isolated local backend: adding completed CGSC 350 increased earned credits from 32 to 35 and updated a pre-existing plan to show SLP 1/8, preserving its unrelated CGSC 375 plan entry. The new requirements page showed 31 total requirements and linked the current catalog.
